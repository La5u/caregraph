Hover or click an item to highlight it · ⓘ What these mean
Using the map
Click a dot or line for its panel. Double-click a dot to gather the nodes linked to it around it (shown ones only; the layout is frozen). Double-click empty space to zoom in. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan or move a dot, F fits all, Esc closes the panel.
Size
Agencies: 2024 Medicare payments plus number of links. People, companies and addresses: number of agencies CMS lists them on, across all networks. Buildings: number of agencies there.
Active
In the latest CMS enrollment file, with no revocation found.
Past
In an earlier enrollment file but not the latest, with no revocation found. This does not by itself mean the agency closed.
Revoked
Listed in the CMS Revocation Extract, shaded by the reason CMS gives, drawn larger, on top, and labeled first. Billing privileges: 42 CFR 424.535(a)(8), "abuse of billing privileges" in CMS's words. Affiliation: (a)(19), an affiliation CMS judged to pose an undue risk. Other: failed site visit (a)(5), failure to report (a)(9), false information (a)(4) and other grounds. Click an agency for the exact text. A revocation can be appealed and later reversed; this shows the CMS extract of 2026-10-21.
Hospice, Home health
A Medicare-enrolled agency, from the CMS Hospice or Home Health Agency enrollment files.
Person, Company
An individual or organization CMS lists on an agency in its All Owners files. Companies are parent, holding and management companies.
Address
A practice address (same suite) CMS lists for two or more agencies.
Building
A whole building whose agencies use different suites, sized and labeled by how many agencies CMS has listed there across the releases. Shown in a network when two of its agencies are there, or when the building has 5 or more agencies.
Owner
5% or greater direct or indirect ownership, or general partner (CMS role codes 34, 35, 38).
Control
Operational or managerial control (code 43): runs the agency, with or without owning it.
Officer
Corporate officer or director (codes 40, 41).
Manager
Managing employee, on payroll (W-2) or contracted (codes 42, 25).
Other
Mortgage, security or limited partnership interest, or a role CMS lists as "other" (codes 36, 37, 39, 44).
Past link
Dashed: not listed in the latest release.
What joins a network
Only owner and control links, and shared addresses where CMS lists both agencies in the same release. Officer, manager and other links, buildings, and faded nodes (generic or very widely listed IDs, shared office centers), are shown but do not join agencies into a network.
